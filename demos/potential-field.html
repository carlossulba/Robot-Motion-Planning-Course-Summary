Attractive/Repulsive Potential Field

Gradient descent on U = U_att + U_rep. Unlike a navigation function, this field can (and sometimes does) trap the robot in a local minimum.

Study material assembled from course summaries, Principles of Robot Motion (Choset, Lynch, Hutchinson, Kantor, Burgard, Kavraki, Thrun), and past exams.
