Trapezoidal Decomposition

A vertical line is extended from every obstacle vertex, slicing free space into trapezoids. Path = adjacency-graph search + centroid/midpoint connectors.

Study material assembled from course summaries, Principles of Robot Motion (Choset, Lynch, Hutchinson, Kantor, Burgard, Kavraki, Thrun), and past exams.
