Bug Algorithms

Bug0 §3.1

Naive strategy: head for the goal, and the moment an obstacle blocks the way, follow its boundary only until a straight step toward the goal is locally free again.

Bug1 §3.2 / book §2.1

Exhaustive strategy: circle the whole obstacle once, remember the best exit point, then retrace to it.

Bug2 §3.3 / book §2.1

Greedy strategy: leave the boundary the instant you cross the fixed start–goal m-line closer to the goal.

Tangent Bug §3.4 / book §2.2

A finite-range radial sensor lets the robot shortcut around obstacles instead of fully committing to boundary-following.

⚠ Known issue: the boundary-following leave condition (dreach < dfollowed) currently fires almost immediately in most runs — measured at 65% of boundary-following episodes leaving within the very first sensed point — so this demo often behaves like the naive Bug0 strategy above instead of showing genuine sensor-driven shortcutting around an obstacle. A fix is pending.
